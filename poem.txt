The Plant's not a sage,
Neither is it a machine,
It requires heed and so,
But without any sarcastic grin.

The heed it need,
Is something natural,
Like the sunshine of the Sun,
Which do not leave it delusional.

Like the UV torch,
Which pretends to be the Sun,
Shows transient photons,
And goes to the UV's chosen.

Yes The Plant want to transpire,
The droplets are just dried out,
The water goes to build The Plant,
Not to some puny spout.

The Plant won't BEG for it,
It can grow without the torch,
The Sun will always be there,
And it won't let it scorch.
