Was living inside ponds and lakes,
Sometimes near seas with waves,
Getting moisture, in its own ways,
Thriving thanks to the sun up and away.

On land were trees and grass,
Surrounded by, tonnes of green mass,
Fed by the same sun, the ultimate source,
Both, the alga and the tree, living each day with a self-surpass!

The alga used to see the reflections
Of the trees and the green lands above.
Perhaps it never cared its own water,
Yet used to live in it.

One day it met a fungus on the bank,
They both had some similar stories,
Formed a symbiosis, a bond strong.
Though never connected, were now together.

Helped each other.
Got a mutual support.
The bond they thought will live forever.
Well, that was a random sport.

The fungus promised,
They'll always be together.
Not in like a hybrid species,
But a symbiosis which won't end, ever!

The alga felt something.
The losing grip of the fungus.
The fungus refused to accept it,
Perhaps the gradual change, didn't matter.

The griped loosened a lot, one day.
The fungus found another alike.
The alga's fungus, started distancing.
The alga, tried to protect the symbiosis.

Alga asked, what changed?
Fungus told, it found alike.
Alga felt, drying out, and felt
The fungus, didn't do right.

The two fungi, formed a hyphae,
Our alga, started returning.
Return to the pond, it once thrived inside.
Now it got a lesson, that not every symbiosis, can thrive.
